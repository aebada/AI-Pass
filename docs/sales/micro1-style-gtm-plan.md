## 1. Thesis

Micro1 grew by pairing **(A) expert human-data services for AI labs/enterprises** with **(B) outbound that pays companies to license operational records**, then turning those records into realistic agent environments.

AI-Pass copies the **operating system** of that GTM, not the paycheck-for-raw-data framing:

| Micro1 lever | AI-Pass equivalent |
|---|---|
| Zara / Experts | Intelligence Lab Experts + twin of roles |
| Flow data platform | Lab Flow + Connect into ERP/CRM/APIs |
| Realms (task environments) | Twin + Realms grounded in customer systems |
| Cortex (agent eval) | Company Harness + Cortex-style confirmation gates |
| Company Data Partnerships (pay for ops data) | **Ops Reality Partnership**: Connect 1–2 systems → governed twin → optional anonymized eval export |
| Multi-channel outbound (email + LinkedIn + call) | Daily 100 email + LinkedIn founder/partner sequence + discovery call |
| Founder/CEO first touch | Partnerships / Innovation / CTO / Founder for mid-market |

We sell **infrastructure that makes company work twin-able and agent-safe**, and we can also sell **Micro1-like expert evaluation / realm services** to AI product teams who need domain fidelity.

## 2. Service catalog (what we offer)

### Track A — Enterprise Twin Pilot (primary for DE industry list)

**Buyer:** CIO / CDO / Innovation / Partnerships / Plant IT  
**Promise:** 15-minute walkthrough → 2–4 week pilot  
**Scope:**

1. Connect 1–2 systems (ERP, CRM, MES, ticketing, docs)
2. Stand up employee / process / machine twin slices
3. Route work through Company Harness with human confirmation on irreversible actions
4. Graph Builder judges facts against evidence (no silent hallucination into the graph)

**Exit:** Pilot report + production quote (Connect seats + harness + twin hosts)

### Track B — Ops Reality Partnership (Micro1 CDP analogue)

**Buyer:** Founder / CEO / Managing Partner (consulting, IT services, MSP, logistics ops)  
**ICP:** 30–200 desk employees, 3+ years operating, 5+ software tools, English or German records  
**Promise:** Turn operational workflows into twin environments *without* selling raw PII; company keeps ownership  
**Value to them:**

- New productized AI offering for *their* clients (white-label twin / harness)
- Or co-build evaluation realms from anonymized SOPs / ticket patterns
- Optional revenue share if realms are licensed to labs later

**Not:** “Send us your CRM dump for a wire transfer.” That is Micro1’s CDP; we only do governed Connect + anonymized task extraction with legal review.

### Track C — Intelligence Lab services (Micro1 data-lab analogue)

**Buyer:** AI lab / agent platform / enterprise AI product team  
**Offer:** Expert-vetted task design, realm construction, agent evaluation rubrics, robotics/edge twin evals  
**Surfaces:** /lab/flow · /lab/realms · /lab/cortex · /lab/experts · /lab/robotics

## 4. Contact strategy (Micro1 playbook)

### Channels (always multi-step)

1. **Email** — daily cap 100 via `scripts/send-daily-outreach.py` (respect Hostinger rate limits; resume from state)
2. **LinkedIn** — same-day connection + note to target_role; day 3 value note; day 7 soft CTA
3. **Call** — only after open/reply or LinkedIn accept; 5-minute discovery script
4. **Warm** — UnternehmerTUM / Women Startup / partner intros for Munich density

### Sequencing rules

- Day 0: mail #1 (wedge + 15 min CTA)
- Day 3: LinkedIn touch (if no reply)
- Day 5: mail #2 (case pattern + risk reduction)
- Day 10: mail #3 (breakup / forward request)
- Stop on “stop”, bounce, or press-only complaint → mark `SKIP`

### Decision-maker targeting

| Segment | First seat | Escalation |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise | Partnerships / Innovation / Alliances | CTO / CDO / Plant IT |
| Mid-market ops | Founder / CEO / Managing Partner | Head of Delivery / Ops |
| AI product | Head of AI / Eval / Product | CTO |

Full copy: `outreach-sequences.md`

## 5. Qualification (before any pilot kickoff)

Must pass **4 of 5**:

1. Named owner who can book a technical counterpart
2. At least one Connect-able system identified
3. Irreversible actions exist (payments, shop-floor writes, customer messages) → harness value clear
4. Data residency / privacy path acceptable (EU / DACH preferred)
5. Budget path for pilot (cash or partner-funded) within 60 days

Kill criteria: press-only inbox with no forward, no systems access in 30 days, “send PDF only” tire-kickers after two touches.

## 6. Discovery → contract → delivery (Micro1 process shape)

4. **Delivery** — twin live in staging; weekly review  
5. **Acceptance** — checklist + production quote
